### D. Whole-Part Reconciliation & Synthesis

**Human-Coded Whole-Part Synthesis (Phase 4)**
*(Transcript: R_1SCQSniQp5NMQAg)*
* **Textural Description (What was experienced):** The participant experienced high school and college marching band as deeply social environments that fostered lifelong feelings of belonging. They rehearsed, performed at football games, and eventually assumed student leadership roles. In high school, the competitive stakes and musical demands were low, meaning the focus remained strictly on participation and enjoyment alongside friends. In college, stepping into roles like drum major introduced the tangible weight of peer reliance. Today, as an adult band director, they actively create challenging and competitive environments for their students, effectively reverse-engineering their own historically unchallenging high school experience.
* **Structural Description (How it was experienced):** The experience was structured by a distinct lack of early rigor, which ironically catalyzed a deeply philosophical pedagogical stance later in life. Belonging was experienced *effortlessly* in high school, and dependability was experienced *tactically* in college leadership. The meaning of their past is inextricably linked to their present; the historical gap in competitive challenge is experienced now as a retroactive deficit that drives their current professional labor. They ensure their students experience the "work that goes into achieving" because they structurally understand what is missing when that rigor is absent.
* **Provisional Statement of Essence:** The essence of the marching experience for this participant is the **retroactive realization of formative gaps**. The lack of profound challenge in a deeply beloved social activity ultimately fostered a pedagogical imperative to construct those exact challenges for the next generation.

**Machine-Analyzed Within-Case Summary (Phase 4 LLM Expansion, GPT-5.2)**
*(Transcript: R_3tz2jIXeKQGZSp1)*
* **Provisional Within-Case Summary:** Across high school marching band, college marching band, and drum corps, the participant describes marching arts as a set of distinct but cumulative experiences that shaped her identity, values, and professional trajectory as a music educator. In high school, she experienced an unusually close, affirming mentor relationship with her director—one that felt deeply supportive and future-oriented. That relationship both validated her capabilities and nudged her toward music education, while the program’s comprehensive, process-focused pedagogy modeled an approach she now finds rare among colleagues. High school was also the first setting where her perfectionism and drive to excel felt meaningfully recognized—not just through achievement, but through visible effort and growth. In college, the most enduring impact was social and developmental: leaving a small town, she found safety, belonging, and lasting friendships through the marching band community, along with formative travel experiences that expanded her world. Drum corps then intensified her relationship to excellence, placing her among “best of the best” peers and immersing her in a culture of shared sacrifice, disciplined teamwork, and leadership responsibilities. Although emotionally taxing—bringing tears and episodes of questioning self-worth—drum corps ultimately reinforced perseverance, growth through process, and a reframed relationship to perfection: ultimate ideals may be unattainable, but sustained work reliably produces improvement. Stepping back, she understands marching arts as a rare synthesis of technical competence, soft skills, leadership, and collective responsibility, where success and failure are shared—an experience she believes uniquely shaped her adult values and sense of community.

## Part 2: Prompts Used in LLM-Assisted Analysis

To ensure methodological transparency, the prompt structures used internally within our analysis scripts are detailed below. We heavily relied on high reasoning model iterations (`gpt-5.2`) and robust system prompt guidelines to align the model with hermeneutic phenomenological practices. 

### A. General Formatting & Framing Prompts

**System Prompt (Framing Prompt for Expansions):**
```text
You are a qualitative research assistant helping a human research team with phenomenological analysis.
```

**Phase 2 Expansion Prompt (Candidate Analytic Memos & Meaning Units):**
```text
Review the following transcript and generate a candidate analytic memo and meaning units for it.
Format your output in Markdown with two sections:
### Candidate Analytic Memo
(Paragraph summarizing the holistic sense of the transcript)
### Candidate Meaning Units
(Numbered list of meaning units capturing phrases, moments, and expressions)

Transcript:
[Insert Text]
```

**Phase 4 Expansion Prompt (Provisional Within-Case Summaries & Themes):**
```text
Review the following transcript and generate a provisional within-case summary and candidate thematic groupings for it.
Format your output in Markdown with two sections:
### Provisional Within-Case Summary
(Paragraph synthesizing the textural and structural meaning)
### Candidate Thematic Groupings
(List of themes with brief descriptions based on the transcript)

Transcript:
[Insert Text]
```

### B. Core Phenomenological API Pass Prompts

The core scripted analysis sequentially prompted the LLM to mirror steps modeled in human coding.

**Pass 1 API Prompt (Meaning Unit Extraction):**
```text
You are a hermeneutic phenomenological researcher.
Perform a holistic reading of the following transcript. Identify "meaning units" that capture phrases, moments, and expressions central to the participant's lived experience of marching arts membership.
Provide output as a JSON object with a key 'meaning_units' which is a list of strings.

Transcript:
[Insert Text]
```

**Pass 2 API Prompt (Thematizing & Structuring):**
```text
You are a hermeneutic phenomenological researcher.
Given the transcript and meaning units, perform interpretive thematizing and whole-part reconciliation. Develop these into textural and structural descriptions reflecting core dimensions of experience.
Output as a JSON object with a key 'themes' containing a list of objects (with 'theme_name' and 'description').

Transcript:
[Insert Text]
Meaning Units:
[Insert Pass 1 Output]
```

**Pass 3 API Prompt (Cross-Narrative Synthesis):**
```text
You are a hermeneutic phenomenological researcher.
Synthesize the following themes from all participants into a phenomenological cross-narrative synthesis. Find overarching patterns and connections. Write a highly eloquent paragraph sharing the main findings and insights into the lived experience ("what is it like to be a marching arts member?").
Output a JSON object with 'cross_narrative_synthesis' and 'findings_paragraph'.

Themes:
[Insert All Participant Themes]
```
